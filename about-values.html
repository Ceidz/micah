Personal values

Honesty, respect, and family are three things I truly value in life. For me, honesty is important because I believe that being truthful helps build trust between people. Respect matters because everyone deserves to be treated properly, regardless of their situation. And of course, family will always have a special place in my heart because they are among the people who support me and stay by my side. I also believe that being a good person isn't just about being there when everything is going well. It's about knowing how to treat others, especially when they're going through difficult times. Even when someone is facing their own struggles, choosing to understand, respect, and support another person can mean a lot. I believe that sometimes, simply staying and making someone feel that they're not alone is already a meaningful thing.
